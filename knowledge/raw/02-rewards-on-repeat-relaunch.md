# CHIPOTLE RELAUNCHES REWARDS WITH "REWARDS ON REPEAT," DELIVERING MORE VALUE WITHOUT TRADE-OFFS

April 13, 2026 — StockTitan / CMG News

## Summary

Chipotle (NYSE: CMG) relaunched Chipotle Rewards as "Rewards on Repeat" effective April 13, 2026, expanding benefits and a redesigned in-app experience across the U.S. and Canada (ex. Quebec).

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Active Rewards members | 21 million |
| Gen Z loyalty signups (2024) | Nearly half |
| Digital transactions linked to Rewards | ~90% |
| In-restaurant transactions linked to Rewards | ~20% |
| Birthday reward redemption window | 30 days |
| Minimum purchase for new-member chips & guac | $5.00 |
| Offer expiration for new-member reward | 7 days |

## Program Highlights

- **21 million** active members
- Monthly **Freepotle** drops to increase member engagement
- Free chips & guac at sign-up
- Extended points expiration (one qualifying purchase/year)
- Lower redemption thresholds
- Expanded offers
- In-restaurant enrollment campaign

## Market Reaction

On the day of the announcement, CMG gained 1.47%, reflecting a mild positive market reaction.

## Financial Context (FY 2025)

- Full-year 2025 revenue: $11.9 billion (5.4% revenue growth)
- Comparable sales fell year-over-year despite revenue growth
